## Brief description
Rubik's Eversion is a browser game that places you at the center of a hollow Rubik's cube, where you rotate your view and turn the face directly in front of you.

## Instructions for use
Click-drag with one finger to rotate the cube around you. After you stop rotating, the view snaps to the nearest face. Use the toolbar buttons to rotate the viewed face, undo, scramble, and reset; desktop trackpads can also use a two-finger twist to rotate the viewed face.
