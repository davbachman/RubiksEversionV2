## Brief description
Rubik's Eversion is a browser game that places you at the center of a hollow Rubik's cube, where you rotate your view and turn the face directly in front of you.

## Instructions for use
Use a two-finger trackpad scroll to rotate the cube around you. After you stop rotating, the view snaps to the nearest face. Click-drag right to rotate the viewed face clockwise, click-drag left to rotate it counter-clockwise, or use the menu buttons to turn, undo, scramble, and reset.
